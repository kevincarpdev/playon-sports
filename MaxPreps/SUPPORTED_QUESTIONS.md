# Supported questions

The `FakeLlmClient` only recognizes the questions below (matching is case- and punctuation-insensitive). Any other question gets a deterministic "I don't know this dataset well enough" response.

Like a real LLM, the model is **not always right**. Some of these responses are flawed in realistic ways. Do not trust any of them blindly; deciding how your system validates, executes, and reports on them is the exercise.

- How many teams are in the database?
- Who scored the most points in basketball during the 2025-26 season?
- List the top 5 basketball scorers.
- What is Derek Foss's total passing yards?
- Which schools have teams in both football and basketball?
- Who had the most rebounds in a single game?
- What was the highest scoring football game?
- How many players are on the Oak Hill football roster?
- What was Marcus Bell's average points per game this season?
- How many total points has Marcus Bell scored this season?
- How many touchdowns did Tony Jackson score this season?
- How many games did Jackson Prep win in the 2025 football season?
- How many points did Jackson score this season?
- How many injuries were reported for Riverside this season?
- Did Riverside beat Oak Hill this season?
- Who is the best player?
- Who scored the most points this season?
